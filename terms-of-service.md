## Using the app responsibly

Reminders depend on your phone: notifications, alarms and battery settings can stop them. Check that your reminders work, and do not rely on the app as the only way you remember important medicines.

Check any medicine the scanner reads, and every dose and time you save, against your prescription. Scanning can make mistakes.

Food and medicine information is general guidance and may be incomplete. Ask your doctor or pharmacist before you start, stop or change a medicine or your diet.

In an emergency, contact emergency services or your doctor, not the app.
