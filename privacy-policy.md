# MedicFood Privacy Policy

_Last updated 18 October 2026_

MedicFood helps you remember your medicines and lets family members help. This policy explains what we collect, why, who sees it, how long we keep it and the choices you have.

## What we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work:

- Account: your email address, your name (or the first part of your email if you have not set one), your profile photo if you sign in with Google, and when you joined and last signed in.
- Your medicines: name, dose, type, timing, duration and instructions, and the days each medicine is scheduled and taken.
- Dose history: for each dose, whether you took, missed or skipped it, and when.
- Family links: who you have linked as a caretaker or look after, what each caretaker may do, and the share code that links accounts.
- Your consent: which version of these documents you accepted and when.
- Optional crash reports, only if you turn them on (see "Crash reports").

## What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, phone number, advertising ID or any government ID. We do not keep the photos you take of prescriptions.

## Prescription photos

When you scan a prescription, the photo is sent to Google's Gemini service so it can read the medicines. The photo is not saved by MedicFood and is deleted from your phone when you close the scanner or retake it. The Gemini API terms from Google govern how it handles the image.

You choose whether to keep the medicines it finds; nothing is saved until you confirm.

## How we use your information

- To show your medicines, set your reminders and show your history and statistics.
- To let the family members you choose see and help with your medicines.
- To sign you in, keep your account secure and send the emails you ask for (email verification and password reset).
- To keep the service running and to fix crashes, if you opted in.

## Medicine and food information

To check medicine names, the app sends the medicine name (not your name or any account information) to public drug databases run by the US National Library of Medicine (RxNorm) and the US FDA (openFDA). The food and drug interaction guidance is a list that ships with the app.

## Who can see your information

Only you can see your account and medicines. A caretaker can see and, if you allow it, change your medicines only after you give them your share code. You can switch a caretaker to view-only or remove them at any time, and their access stops immediately.

We do not sell your information, share it for advertising or show ads.

## Where it is stored and who processes it

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) in Mumbai, India. Google acts as our service provider. Scans are processed by Google's Gemini service. These providers may process data on servers outside India as described in their terms.

## How long we keep it

- Your account and medicines are kept until you delete your account.
- Dose history is deleted automatically after two years.
- When you delete your account, your profile, medicines, dose history, family links and share code are erased, and your sign-in is removed.

## Your choices and rights

You can do the following yourself in Settings > Privacy & Security:

- Download a copy of your data.
- Permanently delete your account and everything in it.
- Turn crash reports on or off.
- Remove a caretaker or limit them to viewing.
- Withdraw your consent by deleting your account.

## Crash reports

Off unless you turn them on. When on, a crash sends the error details, your phone model, Android version and app version, and a random install ID to Firebase Crashlytics. It never includes your medicines or account details.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our providers. Access to your data is limited by strict rules: only verified accounts can use the app, and each person can reach only their own data or data shared with them. No system is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong password and keep your phone locked.

## Children

MedicFood is not directed at children under 18. A parent or guardian may manage medicines for a child by using their own account.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will ask for your agreement again the next time you open the app.

## Contact and complaints

For questions, data requests or complaints, write to medicfood.srmist@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
